# Legacy Refund Policy (v1.0 — Superseded)

## 1. Eligibility Window
Customers may request a full self-service refund within **90 calendar days** of delivery for any hardware item.

## 2. Automatic Approval Threshold
Support agents may issue instant refunds up to **$2,000 USD** without supervisor escalation.

## 3. Restocking Fee
No restocking fee applies to opened hardware returned within 90 days.
